How the route is chosen
  1. Walking time comes from each path's Walk_time ( km/h).
  2. Paths connect wherever they share a point, the same as the ArcGIS Pro network.
  3. Inside a closed building, every path more than 1 m in from the walls is off-limits, so the route goes around.
  4. Drag the A or B marker to place it exactly (a door, a parking spot). Typing or clicking a building puts it back on that building.
  5. Start and end attach to the nearest outdoor path. If that spot is inside a closed building, they move to the nearest walkable spot.
  6. Checked against the ArcGIS Pro tool: Presidents Hall → University Hall at 20:30 is 1,185 m here and 1,186 m in Pro.
Campus closures (academic calendar)

On offices closed days every building is shown closed. On no classes days (breaks) buildings keep their normal hours. Tap a date to see it on the map.
